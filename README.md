## 📂 Available Resources

This repository defines "open-source satellite resources" as publicly shareable knowledge and reference material, rather than satellite software source code or satellite datasets. Its resources may include research reports, industry standards, technical specifications, engineering guidelines, and educational material that help the community understand, design, evaluate, or discuss satellite systems.

The following resources are currently shared in this repository. Each entry records the resource name, type, publisher, version or date, and a short description for discovery and citation.

## Planned Resource Types

Future additions will remain within the repository's non-code, non-dataset scope. Planned categories include:

- **Standards and specifications:** publicly shareable standards, recommendations, interface specifications, and terminology documents relevant to satellite systems, onboard computing, communications, and related engineering activities.
- **Research reports and whitepapers:** reports that analyze satellite technologies, architectures, engineering practices, or standardization progress, including material from research institutions, industry, and open-source communities.
- **Engineering guidelines:** documents covering verification, testing, reliability, safety, security, quality assurance, qualification, and certification practices for satellite-related systems.
- **Educational and reference material:** introductory explanations, glossaries, surveys, and other materials that help students, researchers, and engineers understand satellite technologies and their standards.
